Questions

Everything you might want to ask.

Do you offer free home visits and measurement?
Yes — anywhere in Mumbai, MMR and Navi Mumbai. Our consultant brings fabric swatches, wood samples and shade cards to your home or office. There's no fee and no obligation to buy.
How long does it take to make a custom sofa?
Standard custom sofas ship in 12–18 working days. Larger or fully bespoke pieces take 18–25 days.
Can you repair my old sofa or curtains?
Absolutely — restoration is our speciality. We do re-upholstery, refoaming, frame repair, polishing, curtain re-stitching and roller-blind motor repair. Pickup and drop included within Mumbai.
What's the warranty on your products?
Sofa frames carry a lifetime warranty. Foam, springs, mechanisms and stitching are covered for up to 10 years. Mattresses come with 10-year warranty. Wooden furniture has 5-year structural warranty.
Do you take bulk orders for hotels, offices or builders?
Yes. Orders from 25 to 5,000+ units, with custom branding, GST invoicing and project-level credit. Use the contact form and select "B2B / Bulk".
How do I pay? Is there an EMI option?
UPI, bank transfer, cash and all major cards. EMI on most credit/debit cards (3, 6, 9, 12 months). For B2B orders, 30/60-day credit terms after onboarding.
